We count the cost of every exchange, including ones that don’t replace a new purchase.

How the simulation works

New purchases replaced = completed exchanges × replacement share, rounded down to whole purchases. Monthly savings = (new purchases replaced × average new price) − (all exchanges × average used price or borrowing fee).

Refundable deposits aren’t expenses. Taxes, travel, repairs, and seller income are excluded. This estimates buyer and borrower spending, not net savings for the whole campus economy. A negative result means additional spending.

Sharing can extend an item’s useful life. Carbon savings depend on the item, how long it lasts, and whether it replaces new production; this simulation estimates money only.
