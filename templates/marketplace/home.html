How it works

  1. Say what you need

    Describe it in plain words: size or model, when, and your budget. We propose the details; you confirm them.

  2. See matches with reasons

    Every result is a real listing on campus, with why it fits and what still needs confirming.

  3. Agree on price and deposit

    Settle the fee, the agreed value, and the deposit with the owner in a private exchange. Both of you accept the same terms.

  4. Meet at a public campus spot

    Hand off at a well-lit public place, confirm the condition together, and return the same way.

Prototype note. Deposits and payments are simulated. No real money is held, reserved, or charged.
