These older Pexels photographs are individually marked CC0, rather than assuming
search visibility grants permission. Source pages were inspected on 2026-10-10.
Attribution is also accessible in the application footer. Photography is decorative
nature imagery; neither photo is claimed to depict UNC. Source Sans 3 is a modern
humanist substitute, not a historical Windows font. No Frutiger font is bundled.
Open Sans is the active bundled interface family, with locally installed Segoe UI
preferred to match the user's Archive reference. Source Sans 3 and Carlito
are retained as previous licensed assets. Carlito source and license were inspected directly
at https://github.com/googlefonts/carlito and the Google Fonts OFL directory.
Open Sans attribution, variable axes, and OFL were inspected directly at
https://github.com/googlefonts/opensans and the Google Fonts OFL directory.
It is a modern humanist approximation, not an original Vista font. No new
photographs or generated imagery were added for this typography/material pass.
All interface icons are original inline SVG geometry.
Historical screenshots are reference-only and are not bundled or reproduced.
